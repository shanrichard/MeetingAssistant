# Meeting Assistant 图标

2026-09-21 改为“会议记录页＋单色声波＋笔”，突出会议记录、实时转写和同传助手的用途。蓝色圆角底板上放置一张白色记录页；一组蓝色声波表达音频，下方两条文字线表达转写和总结，右下方斜放的深蓝银色笔强化记录含义。采用简洁的 macOS 风格、浅层次、柔和高光与留白；主体不含文字或聊天气泡。

生成方式：内置 `image_gen`，未调用用户 API Key。工具不提供实际模型标识，不标注特定模型版本。

- 源图：`Resources/Artwork/MeetingAssistant-icon-source.png`（1254 × 1254，带 alpha）。
- 标准 PNG：`Resources/AppIcon.png`（1024 × 1024，带 alpha）。
- macOS 图标：`Resources/AppIcon.icns`（16–1024 像素的标准及 Retina 尺寸）。

运行 `bash scripts/build-icon.sh`，使用系统 `sips` 和 `iconutil` 从源图重新导出，源图保持不变。`Resources/Info.plist` 的 `CFBundleIconFile` 与 `scripts/build-app.sh` 引用这个 ICNS 文件。

## 基础图生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: final macOS application icon for Meeting Assistant, a meeting recorder, live transcription and simultaneous interpretation assistant.
Primary request: a refined Apple-style native macOS icon whose main idea is RECORDED MEETING NOTES AND TWO AUDIO LANGUAGES. It must not look like a messenger or chat app.
Composition: one continuous-corner softly rounded square tile with a restrained soft medium-blue to deep indigo gradient. Center a single upright pearl-white document sheet with subtly rounded corners, a small precise folded upper-right corner, and shallow layered depth. The document is the dominant unmistakable silhouette. In the upper half of this white page, place two compact horizontal audio waveforms stacked with a small gap: one azure-blue waveform and one soft violet waveform, each constructed from five clean rounded vertical strokes. Their similar but slightly different rhythms communicate original speech and interpreted speech. In the lower half of the page place exactly two short left-aligned blue-gray horizontal transcription lines, one a little shorter than the other. Keep all marks bold and simple enough to read at Dock size.
Style: polished native Apple macOS app icon, almost flat front view, precise vector-like geometric forms with subtle dimensional materials, restrained highlights and very soft internal shadows. Calm, professional, minimal. NO balloon forms, NO chat or speech bubbles, NO bubble tails, NO glossy inflated plastic, NO chrome surround, NO dark recessed panel, NO neon, NO glow, NO sparkles, NO tiny details, NO Apple logo, NO microphone emoji, NO typography, letters, numbers, flags, or watermark. Do not add any objects beyond the tile, paper, two waveform rows and two transcript lines.
Production: ONE finished standalone icon, not a mockup or presentation. Square 1024 by 1024 PNG. Main tile about 82 percent of canvas width and height with equal margins on all sides. Genuinely transparent alpha outside the rounded-square tile. Smooth precise antialiased continuous boundary with no stray pixels, no exterior shadow, no baked checkerboard. The white document stays entirely inside the blue tile with ample blue margin.
